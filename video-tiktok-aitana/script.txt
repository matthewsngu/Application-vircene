You think influencers have to be real people. Wrong.
An influencer from Barcelona makes up to ten thousand euros a month. And she doesn't exist.
Her name is Aitana López.
Barcelona, 2023. Rubén Cruz runs a small modeling agency. He's tired of influencers who cancel shoots and have huge egos. So he decides to build his own.
His team gives her three things: pink hair, a personality, and a whole life.
So how does it work?
Every week, the team plans her life: the gym, a concert, a trip. Artificial intelligence generates the photos. Designers fix every detail in Photoshop. Then they write her captions and answer her fans. Brands pay her per post, like any other influencer. The counter climbs: ten thousand euros in a single month.
Over a hundred thousand people follow her. Many of them don't even realize she isn't real.
And here's the twist: a well-known actor slid into her DMs to ask her out. He had no idea she was made by a computer.
So how many of the people you follow are actually real?
