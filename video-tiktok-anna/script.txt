You think you need money to live like a millionaire. Wrong.
A twenty-five-year-old talked her way into New York's fanciest hotels, restaurants and banks with almost no money.
Her name was Anna Delvey.
Except it wasn't. She was Anna Sorokin, born near Moscow in 1991, and she moved to a small German town as a teenager. In 2013, she landed in New York with a new name and a new story: a German heiress with a sixty-million-dollar trust fund.
Her act had three parts: designer clothes, hundred-dollar tips, and a plan for a private art club on Park Avenue.
So how did it work?
Pure confidence. She lived in luxury hotels and promised to pay later. She showed banks fake documents and asked for a twenty-two-million-dollar loan. A friend even got stuck with a sixty-two-thousand-dollar bill from a trip to Morocco. The bill climbs: two hundred seventy-five thousand dollars.
October 2017. Police arrest her in a sting. In 2019, a jury convicts her of grand larceny.
And here's the twist: Netflix paid her three hundred twenty thousand dollars for her story.
So be honest: would you have believed her?
